Trading leveraged financial instruments (forex, futures, CFDs, indices, crypto) carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for everyone. You can lose more than you expect, including the entire fee paid for a challenge or evaluation.

  • Most retail traders lose money. Past performance and any firm's published payout statistics do not guarantee future results.
  • Prop-firm "funded" accounts are often simulated; payouts depend on the firm's terms, which can change or be disputed.
  • Drawdown, consistency, news-trading, and payout rules vary and are strictly enforced; breaching a rule can end an account.
  • Leverage magnifies both gains and losses.
  • Calculators on Firm Garage are simplified estimates, use inputs you provide, and may not reflect a firm's exact rule set, fees, or spreads.

Only risk money you can afford to lose and seek independent financial advice. Firm Garage does not provide investment advice.
