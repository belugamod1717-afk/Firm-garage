Firm Garage ("we", "us") is operated by [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS]. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights. Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, country, and a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider; we never see it).
  • Content you create: favorites, saved comparisons, trading-journal entries, firm reviews, and tool inputs you choose to save.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser/device type, and security logs (login attempts, MFA events) used to protect accounts.
  • Essential storage: a login-session token and your theme preference in your browser. We do not use advertising trackers.

2. Why we use it (legal bases)

  • To provide your account and features — contract.
  • To secure the service and prevent fraud/abuse — legitimate interests.
  • To send account emails (verification, password reset) — contract/legitimate interests.
  • To meet legal obligations — legal obligation.

3. Service providers

We use: Supabase (authentication and database), Vercel (hosting), Google Fonts and Google's favicon service (fonts and firm logos — Google receives your IP address when these load), and esm.sh (code delivery). Each processes data under its own terms. Some may process data outside your country; where required we rely on appropriate safeguards.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Reviews you publish are visible to others. We may disclose data if legally required or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention

We keep account data while your account is active and delete or anonymise it within [30] days of a deletion request, except where law requires longer retention. Security logs are kept for [90] days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may access, correct, export, or delete your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL]; we respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level access controls so users can only access their own records, optional multi-factor authentication, and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure; use a unique password and enable MFA.

8. Children

Firm Garage is for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
